How Commercial Loan and Foreclosure Cases End in Texas Courts

152 written court opinions in Texas commercial loan and foreclosure cases from 2001 to 2026: 95 from state appeals courts, 30 from bankruptcy courts, 13 from federal district courts. The most common properties were multifamily, hospitality, land/development, retail.

What the opinions show

Lenders' claims

69% of trial results on these claims were affirmed on appeal (35 appealed claims). The rest were reversed, modified or sent back.

Borrowers' and guarantors' claims

55% of trial results on these claims were affirmed on appeal (60 appealed claims). The rest were reversed, modified or sent back.

Source: 152 written opinions in commercial real estate cases from state and federal courts, 2000 to 2026, in CourtListener's free public archive of court decisions. Each opinion was reviewed, and a dollar figure counts only if the opinion's own words state it. A second review of 402 cases agreed on the case type 94% of the time and on award amounts 93% of the time. Written opinions mostly come from cases that were fought to a decision and appealed; most disputes settle without one, so these figures describe contested cases. No case is named here.
