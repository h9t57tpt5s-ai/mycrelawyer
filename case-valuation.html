Case Value Calculator

Beta — modeling in active development

A structured, rules-based estimate of litigation exposure across 8 commercial real estate categories — probability range × damages range, never a single number, and every result traces to a specific rule or a real cited case below it. Describe your case in your own words and/or drop in your pleadings — AI identifies the type of dispute, extracts the facts, and writes up the reasoning for you.

How This Works

This tool is in beta. The underlying probability and damages modeling is still being actively developed and refined as more real case data — settlements, judgments, and consent decrees — is incorporated. Ranges shown today should be treated as an early, evolving estimate, not a finished or stable model.

This is a transparent, explainable framework calibrated against real, cited case outcomes and (where relevant) state law — not a black-box statistical prediction, and not a substitute for judicial or arbitral resolution of an actual dispute. Every probability and damages range shown is grounded in either a specific legal rule or a comparable case cited in the results.

This tool does not provide legal advice and does not predict the outcome of any specific matter. Litigation outcomes depend on facts, evidence, jurisdiction, and counsel that a short questionnaire cannot capture. Discuss an actual matter with counsel →
