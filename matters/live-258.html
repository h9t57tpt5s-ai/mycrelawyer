Wiggins v. Cedar Christina Crossing, LLC: Delaware Supreme Court Affirms Summary Judgment in Parking-Lot Ice Fall

Delaware's Supreme Court affirmed summary judgment for a shopping-center owner and its snow-removal contractor in a parking-lot black-ice fall, after security video showed the lot clear and dry.

The Delaware Supreme Court affirmed summary judgment for a shopping-center owner and its snow-removal contractor in a suit over a black-ice fall in a parking lot, holding that a plaintiff cannot rely on self-contradicted testimony to defeat summary judgment once the record shows reasonable snow-and-ice care. Security video showed the lot clear and dry on the day in question, directly contradicting the plaintiff's own deposition testimony describing unplowed snow.

Why it matters

The ruling confirms that Delaware shopping-center owners and their maintenance contractors can defeat weather-related slip-and-fall claims at summary judgment using objective video evidence, reinforcing the litigation-risk value of parking-lot camera coverage and routine snow-and-ice documentation for commercial landlords.
