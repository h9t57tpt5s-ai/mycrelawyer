Freddie Mac v. Nord Group Affiliate (Edgewater Apartments Post-Foreclosure Rent Diversion Dispute)

A federal magistrate judge faulted a Nord Group unit for collecting rent on the 228-unit Edgewater Apartments in Lake Jackson, Texas after Freddie Mac foreclosed, then suing its lender instead of turning it over.

Trade press reported October 15, 2026 that U.S. Magistrate Judge Andrew M. Edison called it "pretty damn egregious" that a Nord Group subsidiary kept collecting rent on Edgewater Apartments, a 228-unit Lake Jackson, Texas complex, after Freddie Mac had already foreclosed on the property, and then sued its own lender over the dispute rather than turning over the proceeds. The report describes a broader pattern across Nord Group's multifamily portfolio of lenders and investors accusing Nord entities of diverting rents, submitting falsified insurance records, and letting buildings deteriorate, with court filings describing sewage backups, mold, and vermin at some properties.

Why it matters

The case illustrates how a borrower's continued control over a foreclosed asset's cash flow, and an offensive lawsuit against the foreclosing lender, can expose a distressed sponsor to conversion and unjust-enrichment claims well beyond an ordinary loan default, while allegations of falsified insurance certifications can independently trigger recourse carve-outs in a non-recourse loan. Lenders and special servicers holding 2021-2022 vintage multifamily loans should treat the matter as a reminder to secure receivership and audit borrower-reported insurance and property conditions promptly once a maturity default occurs.

A federal magistrate judge in the Southern District of Texas did not mince words at a recent hearing over a 228-unit apartment complex outside Houston: the conduct he was being asked to evaluate, he said from the bench, was "pretty damn egregious." The remark, from U.S. Magistrate Judge Andrew M. Edison of the court's Galveston Division, came in a dispute between Freddie Mac and a subsidiary of New York-based multifamily buyer Nord Group over Edgewater Apartments, a 228-unit garden-style community in Lake Jackson, Texas that Nord acquired in 2022. According to reporting on the case, Freddie Mac had already foreclosed on the property after the Nord subsidiary stopped making mortgage payments roughly eighteen months earlier, yet the former owner kept collecting rent from tenants after losing title, and then sued its own lender over the dispute rather than relinquish the proceeds.
