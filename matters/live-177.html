Full write-up

A downtown Seattle retail property owned by an entity linked to Unico Properties has been placed in receivership after a default on a $14.5 million loan that matured two years earlier, the Puget Sound Business Journal reported, according to Connect CRE's September 24, 2026 lender roundup. King County Superior Court Commissioner Sarah Moen appointed a Trigild representative as custodial receiver for Unico's retail condominium units at 1419 First Ave., next to Pike Place Market. The appointment followed an agreement between Unico and lender HFX Funding LLC.

Unico markets the property as First and Union, a 44,000-square-foot, three-story retail condominium built in 1985 and renovated in 2011, according to its website. The Registry reported on September 17, 2026 that the property's occupancy fell after Pike Brewing Company closed its 30-year flagship taproom there in November 2025.
