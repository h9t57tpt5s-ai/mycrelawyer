U.S. Bank, as trustee for a 2016 CMBS trust, sued to foreclose on an office complex in Princeton Pike Corporate Center in Lawrence Township, NJ after a $130M loan matured unpaid, and seeks a receiver.

U.S. Bank National Association, as trustee for a 2016 Morgan Stanley Bank of America Merrill Lynch commercial mortgage trust, sued Lenox Drive Office Park LLC on August 12 to foreclose on an office complex within the Princeton Pike Corporate Center in Lawrence Township, Mercer County, New Jersey, after a $130M loan originated in December 2015 matured unpaid on January 1, 2026. The complaint seeks a judicial foreclosure and sale of the property, plus immediate appointment of a receiver to operate the complex while the case proceeds.

Why it matters

A clean maturity-default case, not a covenant or performance dispute, illustrating how 2015-2016-vintage CMBS office loans are colliding with a refinancing market that no longer supports their original terms. The trust's request for a receiver ahead of any foreclosure sale shows special servicers moving to seize operational control of underperforming office collateral faster than a contested foreclosure alone would allow, a pattern owners of similar-vintage suburban office loans should read as an early-warning template.
