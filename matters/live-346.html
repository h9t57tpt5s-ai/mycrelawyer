Fannie Mae sued SYS Augusta LLC in Georgia federal court, saying the owner of Falcon Crest Apartments defaulted on a $21.45 million loan after missing payments and now faces a separate condemnation action on the property.

Fannie Mae filed suit on October 22, 2026 in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Georgia against SYS Augusta LLC, the single-asset owner of Falcon Crest Apartments in Augusta, alleging the borrower missed monthly payments of roughly $103,138.75 for August through October 2026 after an already-late July payment. Fannie Mae accelerated the full $21.45 million loan balance on September 1, 2026, and the complaint also says the defaults coincided with a separate condemnation proceeding against the property.

Why it matters

The filing shows an agency lender moving quickly from a payment default to full acceleration and litigation once a multifamily property is also facing a local condemnation or habitability issue, rather than extending forbearance. Owners and asset managers of older multifamily stock should treat early code-enforcement or condemnation inquiries as a signal to engage lenders before a missed payment converts a maintenance problem into an accelerated, immediately-due debt.
