Rialto Capital Advisors v. Jenel Management Corp. ($17.7M Loop Retail Condo Foreclosure)

Lending & Foreclosure Filed

April 1, 2026

Amount / scale

Parties
Plaintiff: Rialto Capital Advisors

Rialto Capital Advisors, as special servicer for a securitized commercial mortgage loan trust, filed suit on April 1, 2026 in the Circuit Court of Cook County to foreclose on a roughly 20,211-square-foot retail condominium occupying the first two floors of a 21-story tower at 36 South State Street. The complaint alleges landlord Jenel Management Corp. and affiliated entities defaulted on the loan's scheduled maturity, with Rialto now claiming approximately $17.7 million including default interest and liquidation fees; a Cook County hearing is set for September 28, 2026.

Why it matters

A maturity-default foreclosure on ground-floor retail, an asset type once viewed as more resilient than office, signals that the Loop's distressed-debt cycle now extends beyond office towers. CRE lenders, special servicers, and downtown retail owners should treat a strong operating history as no guarantee against maturity-wall refinancing failure in the current rate environment.

A CMBS special servicer has filed suit to foreclose on a two-floor retail condominium at the base of a 21-story tower on Chicago's State Street, the latest sign that loan maturity defaults continue to work through the Loop's distressed commercial debt even in property types that once looked more resilient than office. Rialto Capital Advisors, acting as special servicer for a securitized commercial mortgage loan trust, filed the foreclosure action on April 1, 2026 in the Circuit Court of Cook County, Illinois, against Jenel Management Corp. and affiliated Jenel Real Estate entities, the New York-based landlord that owns the roughly 20,211-square-foot retail condominium occupying the first two floors of the tower at 36 South State Street, at the corner of State and Monroe.
