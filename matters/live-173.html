Computershare Trust Co. v. Showboat Properties LLC (CMBS Foreclosure on Atlantic City's Showboat)

Lending & Foreclosure Filed
Date
June 30, 2026
Added to tracker
September 23, 2026

Superior Court of New Jersey, Chancery Division, Atlantic County
State
New Jersey
Property type
Hospitality
Amount / scale
$48.4 million CMBS loan alleged in default; trustee seeks judgment permitting a sheriff's sale
Parties
Trustee/Plaintiff: Computershare Trust Co.Defendant/Borrower: Showboat Properties LLC

Computershare, as CMBS trustee, seeks to foreclose on part of Atlantic City's Showboat hotel complex, including at least 475 rooms and the garage, over a $48.4 million loan unpaid for over a year.

Computershare Trust Co., as trustee for CMBS investors, filed a foreclosure complaint on June 30, 2026 against Showboat Properties LLC and entities tied to developer Bart Blatstein, alleging no payments have been made on a $48.4 million loan secured by part of Atlantic City's Showboat hotel complex in over a year. The complaint targets at least 475 guest rooms, the property's arcade, and its parking garage, and seeks a court-ordered sale of the collateral.

Why it matters

Illustrates how CMBS trust structures leave borrowers with far less workout flexibility than a balance-sheet lender once a monetary default occurs, and shows how unresolved contractor and mechanic's lien disputes tied to renovation work can compound a hospitality owner's distress alongside a loan default. Special servicers and receivers stepping into similar assets should expect to resolve ancillary contractor claims as part of, not separate from, the disposition process.

Atlantic City's Showboat, one of the Boardwalk's most recognizable hotel properties, is now the subject of a commercial foreclosure action after its mortgage trustee accused the ownership entity controlled by developer Bart Blatstein of defaulting on a $48.4 million commercial mortgage-backed securities loan. Computershare Trust Co., acting on behalf of investors holding the CMBS trust that owns the loan, filed the foreclosure complaint on June 30, 2026 in the Superior Court of New Jersey, Chancery Division, Atlantic County, naming Showboat Properties LLC and related Blatstein entities as defendants. The trustee alleges the borrower has made no payments toward the debt in over a year and is seeking a judgment permitting a sheriff's sale of the mortgaged portion of the property.
