Acres Loan Origination LLC v. DMG Investments Affiliate (Auden Buffalo Student Housing Foreclosure)

Parties
Plaintiff: Acres Loan Origination LLC

Acres Loan Origination sued to foreclose on the Auden Buffalo student housing community in Amherst, NY, alleging $32.5 million in 2021 construction loans to a DMG Investments affiliate matured unpaid.

Acres Loan Origination LLC, an affiliate of commercial mortgage lender Acres Capital, filed a foreclosure complaint in New York state Supreme Court against the Auden Buffalo student housing community at 2915-2949 N. Forest Road in Amherst, Buffalo Business First reported, according to Connect CRE's August 13, 2026 roundup. Acres says it made two loans totaling $32.5 million in early 2021 to build the property, and that the loan matured in January 2024 with the entire principal unpaid. The defendant is an affiliate of New York City developer DMG Investments, which days earlier faced a separate foreclosure filing by a lender group represented by Deutsche Bank National Trust Co. over the Air Buffalo apartment building at 1265 Sweet Home Rd., also owned by a DMG affiliate.

Why it matters

The Auden Buffalo complaint is the second foreclosure action within days against a DMG affiliate's Amherst apartment property, brought by a different lender. Acres describes a maturity default: construction debt that came due in January 2024 and, it says, remained entirely unpaid.

Full write-up

Acres Loan Origination LLC, an affiliate of commercial mortgage lender Acres Capital, has filed a foreclosure complaint in New York state Supreme Court against the Auden Buffalo student housing community at 2915-2949 N. Forest Road in Amherst, Buffalo Business First reported, according to Connect CRE's roundup for the week of August 13, 2026. The defendant is an affiliate of DMG Investments, a New York City real estate developer.

In the complaint, Acres says it made two loans totaling $32.5 million in early 2021 to finance construction of Auden Buffalo, and that the loan matured in January 2024 with all of the principal still unpaid.

The filing came days after a group of lenders represented by Deutsche Bank National Trust Co. began foreclosure proceedings against Air Buffalo, an apartment building at 1265 Sweet Home Rd. that is also owned by a DMG affiliate, according to the same report. Connect CRE's summary did not include a response from DMG.
