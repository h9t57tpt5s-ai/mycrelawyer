The Oklahoma Department of Securities sued Vesta Realty, LLC on August 24, 2026, alleging the Tulsa-based multifamily company failed to provide subpoenaed documents, including the identities of and communications with investors in some of the limited liability companies under the Vesta umbrella, Oklahoma Watch reported. The department has asked the district court to order the records produced. CEO Marc Kulick told Oklahoma Watch the company is 'in the process of engaging an attorney' and looks forward to resolving the matter.

Why it matters

The case is a civil subpoena-enforcement action, though the department's statute allows criminal referrals and information sharing with enforcement agencies. It adds a state regulator to the private suits already filed against Vesta and Kulick.

Full write-up

The Oklahoma Department of Securities has sued Vesta Realty, LLC, alleging the Tulsa-based multifamily company did not provide internal documents the department subpoenaed, Oklahoma Watch reported on October 1, 2026. The suit, filed August 24, 2026, says the subpoena sought records including the identities of, and communications with, investors in a portion of the limited liability companies under the Vesta umbrella.

Shaun Mullins, a trial attorney with the department, told Oklahoma Watch the department did not receive the documents and has asked the district court for an order requiring their production. The case is civil, but Mullins said the department's statute allows it to make criminal referrals and share investigative information with agencies that handle criminal enforcement; he said he could not comment on whether either had happened.

Kulick told Oklahoma Watch he was aware of the suit. 'We are in the process of engaging an attorney and look forward to getting this matter resolved,' he said in a text. Oklahoma Watch counted at least 28 lawsuits filed in 2026 against Kulick, his company or a property he controlled, including a suit by a business partner alleging Kulick used the business as a personal piggy bank.
