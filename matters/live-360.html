Kansas Court Orders Sheriff's Sale of Stalled Mission Gateway Site After $46.24M Judgment

After Aryeh Realty missed an Oct. 16 deadline to pay a $46.24 million judgment, Metropolitan Commercial Bank sought and a Kansas court ordered a sheriff's sale of the vacant Mission Gateway site.

Metropolitan Commercial Bank, which sued owner Aryeh Realty LLC in 2023 over a defaulted $26 million loan on the Mission Gateway site in Mission, Kansas, obtained a $46.24 million final judgment on Oct. 2, 2026 covering the loan default and unpaid property taxes, KCTV5 reported. After Aryeh missed an Oct. 16 payoff deadline, the bank asked the court on Oct. 26 for a sheriff's sale, and a Johnson County judge ordered the sale in court documents filed Oct. 30. No sale date had been set.

Why it matters

The case shows the steps between a foreclosure judgment and a change of ownership on a long-stalled redevelopment site: a payoff deadline, a sale request, a sheriff's sale order and the sale itself. The City of Mission's agreement with a prospective buyer depends on how that process ends.

Full write-up

The former Mission Mall site in Mission, Kansas, a 17-acre tract known as Mission Gateway that has sat empty for about 20 years, is headed to a sheriff's sale. Metropolitan Commercial Bank sued owner Aryeh Realty LLC, a New York company that the Johnson County Post identified as a subsidiary of the Cameron Group, in 2023, saying Aryeh failed to repay about $24 million on a $26 million loan dating to 2019. In December 2025, after closing arguments on Dec. 9, Johnson County District Court Judge Robert Wonnell granted the bank's request to proceed with a foreclosure sale while other legal issues were resolved, the Post reported.

On Friday, Oct. 2, 2026, the court entered a final judgment requiring Aryeh to pay $46.24 million for the loan default and unpaid property taxes and gave it until Oct. 16 to pay before the bank could request a sale by the Johnson County Sheriff, KCTV5 reported. After the deadline passed, the bank's attorney filed the request on Monday, Oct. 26, and in court documents filed Friday, Oct. 30, a judge ordered the sheriff's sale, according to KCTV5. No sale date had been announced.

In early October, the City of Mission announced an agreement with the R.H. Johnson Company that "contemplates the acquisition of the former Mission Mall site," KCTV5 reported. That agreement depends on the outcome of the foreclosure proceedings.
