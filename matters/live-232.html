Why it matters

The suit seeks to make a city, and insurers Fox says covered it decades ago, pay for a cleanup that a later owner was ordered to perform. The allegations have not been tested in court.

Full write-up

Fox Investments LLC filed a complaint on July 23, 2026 in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin against the City of Kaukauna and insurers Sentry, The Travelers Indemnity Company and Employers Insurance Company of Wausau, plus a placeholder "ABC Insurance Company" for any carrier not yet identified, Insurance Business reported. The property, at 400 Fox Shores Drive, is the Fox Shore Apartments complex, which Fox and affiliated parties owned in whole or in part from about February 1998 to January 2020.

Fox alleges the city owned and operated the site from 1967 to 1974, when it was part of a former railyard with a roundhouse, after buying it to clear for redevelopment. It alleges, on information and belief, that the city spread contamination across the property and adjoining land when it burned the structures in November 1974. In 2018, a lender's Phase II Environmental Site Assessment found heavy metals, including arsenic, cadmium, lead, selenium and mercury, in the soil and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons in the groundwater at levels the filing says exceeded state standards.

Fox reported the contamination to the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources under the state's Spill Law. On January 17, 2019, the DNR named Fox a responsible party and directed it to investigate and clean up the site, which it did through Stantec Consulting; the DNR closed the site on May 10, 2022. Fox seeks $235,683.63 in investigation and remediation costs.

Fox claims the city is strictly liable under CERCLA as a past owner or operator at the time of disposal, and also brings negligence and unjust-enrichment claims, alleging the city later hired Stantec and benefited from work Fox paid for. Under Wisconsin's direct-action statute, Wis. Stat. § 632.24, it sues insurers it alleges, on information and belief, covered the city during its ownership; the filing does not identify policy periods or numbers. Insurance Business reported no response from the defendants.
