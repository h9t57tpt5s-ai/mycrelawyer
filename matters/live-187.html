After suing over a $97.3 million loan default, ACORE Capital took control of Salt Lake City's Industry SLC office campus and has relaunched it as Foundry43 with Lincoln Property Company.

ACORE Capital and Lincoln Property Company have relaunched Industry SLC, a 230,000-square-foot office campus in Salt Lake City's Granary District, as Foundry43, with JLL leading leasing, Building Salt Lake reported on September 24, 2026. ACORE Capital Mortgage sued in Utah's Business and Chancery Court in fall 2025, alleging Catalyst Opportunity Funds had defaulted on a $97.3 million loan; the property went into receivership and a foreclosure sale was scheduled for early March 2026. A deed signed March 9 and recorded June 1 conveyed the parcels from ACORE to Marwood Investment Trust, a Delaware statutory trust.

Full write-up

The Granary District office campus formerly known as Industry SLC has been renamed Foundry43, Building Salt Lake reported on September 24, 2026. Lincoln Property Company and lender ACORE Capital announced the relaunch, and JLL is leading leasing. The parking structure and retail space are complete, and work continues on more than 64,000 square feet of creative office space and a 34,000-square-foot fitness center. The announcement describes a 230,000-square-foot campus and 12,000 square feet of retail to be added at the 1,000-stall garage.

Industry SLC, which opened in 2020 in a converted 1943 foundry, was widely seen as a catalyst for the Granary District. After developer Makers Line collapsed in 2023, Catalyst Opportunity Funds took over the office building and the unfinished garage. In fall 2025, ACORE Capital Mortgage sued Catalyst in Utah's Business and Chancery Court, alleging it had defaulted on a $97.3 million loan that September. The property went into receivership, garage construction stalled, and a foreclosure sale was scheduled for early March 2026.

According to Building Salt Lake, ACORE conveyed the parcels at 513 and 517 W. 600 South to Marwood Investment Trust, a Delaware statutory trust, by a deed signed March 9 and recorded June 1, 2026.
