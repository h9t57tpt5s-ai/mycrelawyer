Full write-up

Wilmington Trust National Association filed suit on February 2, 2024 in Marion Superior Court to foreclose on Regions Tower, the 36-story office tower at 211 N. Pennsylvania St. in downtown Indianapolis, the Indianapolis Business Journal reported. The IBJ described Wilmington as acting as trustee, with Cleveland-based KeyBank as the debt servicer and Morgan Stanley Mortgage Capital Holdings LLC as the loan's owner. The defendants are owner entities NG 211 N. Pennsylvania St LLC and Regions Tower MF LLC; New York-based The Nightingale Group bought the tower in 2014 for $65 million.

The suit says the owners missed an October 1, 2023 deadline to pay off the outstanding balance, and WISH-TV reported the owners owe $74,894,311.90. Wilmington asks for immediate repayment with interest and legal fees or, alternatively, foreclosure and a sheriff's sale, and asks the court to appoint a receiver from Riveron RTS LLC. JLL's Indianapolis office asked to keep its role as property manager.

Completed in 1970 as Indiana National Bank's headquarters, the roughly 685,000-square-foot tower was Indiana's tallest building until 1982. It was about 75% occupied according to the building's website, and Regions Financial Corp. houses its Indiana operations there as a tenant, the IBJ reported. The paper called it at least the second major downtown office building to face foreclosure in six months, after Circle Tower. Neither outlet reported a response from the owners, and we have not found later reporting on a receivership or sale.
