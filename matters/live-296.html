Rubenstein et al. v. Millrose Properties, Inc.: Investors Sue Lennar Land-Bank Spinoff Over Undisclosed Single-Family Rental Business

Investors filed a securities class action against Lennar land-bank spinoff Millrose Properties, alleging it hid roughly $200 million of Lennar home purchases for rental; its shares fell 8.5% on the disclosure.

Investors filed a federal securities fraud class action on October 9, 2026 against Millrose Properties, Inc. — the land-banking REIT Lennar spun off in February 2025 — and its CEO and CFO, alleging the company misrepresented its business as pure land-holding while secretly buying roughly $200 million of completed Lennar homes and renting them out. The suit, covering purchasers of Millrose stock between February 7, 2025 and October 2, 2026, follows an October 2, 2026 Hunterbrook Media report that identified more than 700 such home purchases across 51 counties in 15 states; Millrose shares fell 8.5% on the news.

Why it matters

The case tests how much disclosure a spun-off REIT owes the market when an amendment to its governing agreements with its former parent quietly expands its business beyond the narrow mandate investors were sold on at the IPO. For land-banking vehicles, ground-lease REITs, and other single-purpose spinoffs, it is an early signal that courts may treat a related-party contract amendment as a disclosure event in its own right, not a private matter between affiliates.

Investors have filed a federal securities fraud class action against Millrose Properties, Inc., the Miami-based land-banking REIT spun off from homebuilder Lennar Corporation in February 2025, alleging that the company and two of its top executives misrepresented its business model by secretly acquiring and renting out completed single-family homes after telling the market it would hold undeveloped land and nothing more. The case, Rubenstein et al. v. Millrose Properties, Inc. et al., No. 9:26-cv-81386, was filed October 9, 2026 in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida on behalf of investors who purchased Millrose stock between February 7, 2025 and October 2, 2026.
