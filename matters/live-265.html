The owner of the 203-room Wellsworth Hotel in Southbridge, Massachusetts filed Chapter 11 one day before lender X-Caliber Rural Capital's foreclosure auctions on its $13.25 million loan.

Southbridge Associates LLC, which owns the 203-room Wellsworth Hotel and conference center complex at 14 Mechanic Street in Southbridge, Massachusetts, filed a Chapter 11 petition on October 6, 2026. The filing came one day before October 7 foreclosure auctions of the hotel site and the on-site power plant equipment that had been scheduled on behalf of lender X-Caliber Rural Capital, which made a $13.25 million loan on the property in 2022. A cash collateral motion was docketed on October 8, and an affiliate, Southbridge Associates II LLC, had already filed its own Chapter 11 case on September 21.

Why it matters

The case is a textbook eve-of-auction filing: the automatic stay generally freezes a mortgagee's sale the moment the petition is filed, shifting the fight from the auction block to the bankruptcy court's cash collateral and stay-relief hearings. Hotel lenders should note how quickly the cash collateral fight arrives, since room and event revenue is both the lender's collateral and the debtor's operating budget.

Southbridge Associates LLC filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Massachusetts on October 6, 2026 (Case No. 26-41208). The petition lists its principal assets at 14 Mechanic Street in Southbridge, the former American Optical Company factory campus that now houses the Wellsworth Hotel. According to Banker & Tradesman, the 10-acre site includes a 203-room hotel, about 19,400 square feet of meeting and event space, an auditorium, and roughly 72,400 square feet of office space, only part of which is leased. The petition estimates liabilities of $10 million to $50 million and assets of no more than $50,000, and states that funds will be available for unsecured creditors.
