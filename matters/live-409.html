First Tech Federal Credit Union Sets Foreclosure Auction of Westborough Shopping Center Over $32.4M Mortgage

A credit union lender has scheduled a Dec. 8 foreclosure auction of the 33.5-acre, Stop & Shop- and Staples-anchored Westborough Shopping Center on Route 9, citing a breach of a mortgage last amended to $32.375 million in July 2025.

First Technology Federal Credit Union, formerly Digital Federal Credit Union, has scheduled a mortgagee's foreclosure auction of the Westborough Shopping Center on Route 9 in Westborough, Massachusetts, for December 8, 2026 at 11 a.m., MassLive reported. Registry records cited by MassLive show owner Colangelo Massachusetts Real Estate LLC took a $30.75 million mortgage from the credit union in December 2022 that was updated to $32.375 million on July 1, 2025. The lender's notice of sale says the owner breached the mortgage's conditions.

Why it matters

The center is anchored by a Stop & Shop grocery store, and the mortgage debt exceeds the roughly $28.71 million at which the town assesses its two parcels. The sale is proceeding under the mortgage's power of sale rather than through a foreclosure lawsuit.

Full write-up

The Westborough Shopping Center on Route 9 in Westborough, Massachusetts, is scheduled for a foreclosure auction on December 8, 2026 at 11 a.m. in the Staples parking lot, MassLive reported, citing the listing by auctioneer Paul E. Saperstein Co. The mortgagee's notice of sale identifies the holder of the mortgage as First Technology Federal Credit Union, formerly known as Digital Federal Credit Union.

The auction listing describes 18-30 Lyman Street as 33.5 acres in two parcels, improved with about 371,425 square feet of net rentable area in five principal structures, including a multi-tenant shopping plaza, stand-alone buildings and a commercial office building, with about 1,208 parking spaces. Stop & Shop and Staples are the anchor tenants; MassLive also lists a Subway and a Supercuts.

MassLive, citing the Worcester County Registry of Deeds, reported that Colangelo Massachusetts Real Estate LLC owns the property and took out a $30.75 million mortgage from Digital Federal Credit Union in December 2022, which was updated to $32.375 million on July 1, 2025. The notice of sale, which refers to an amended and restated mortgage dated July 1, 2025, says the property will be sold under the mortgage's power of sale for breach of its conditions; it does not describe the breach. The two parcels carry town assessments totaling about $28.71 million. No response from the owner was reported.

Under the posted terms, the buyer must bring a $100,000 deposit by bank or certified check to the sale, pay 10 percent of the price within ten days and pay the balance within 30 days. Other terms are to be announced at the sale.
