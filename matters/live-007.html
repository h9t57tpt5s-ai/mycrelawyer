Via Mizner Lender 1, agent for a lender group, sued to foreclose on the unfinished Mandarin Oriental condo tower in Boca Raton, seeking $417.7M and priority over contractor and buyer claims.

Via Mizner Lender 1 LLC, administrative agent for a group of lenders, filed a foreclosure complaint in Palm Beach County Circuit Court on July 17, 2026 against Via Mizner Owner III, the Penn-Florida Companies affiliate building the Mandarin Oriental condominiums in Boca Raton, The Coastal Star reported. The lenders seek $417.7 million to repay an original $270 million loan, secured by an August 2023 mortgage, plus interest, advances and fees, and allege the borrower stopped paying interest in June 2024 and did not pay off the loan in September 2025 as promised. The complaint asks the court to rank the mortgage ahead of claims by 'purported junior lienholders,' including construction manager Strategic Group Builders, and 'purported contract purchasers' of units.

Why it matters

The case sets a senior construction lender's mortgage against a contractor's recorded lien and the claims of unit buyers on an unfinished condominium project, while a related hotel project is separately in Chapter 11. How the court ranks those claims will matter to lenders, contractors and buyers on other unfinished projects.

Full write-up

Via Mizner Lender 1 LLC, the administrative agent for a group of lenders, sued in Palm Beach County Circuit Court on July 17, 2026 to foreclose a mortgage on the Mandarin Oriental condominium project under construction on Federal Highway in Boca Raton, according to The Coastal Star. The borrower is Via Mizner Owner III, the Penn-Florida Companies affiliate building the condos, which gave the mortgage in August 2023. The lenders seek $417.7 million to repay the original $270 million loan plus interest, advances and fees, and allege Penn-Florida stopped making interest payments in June 2024 and failed to pay the full amount in September 2025 as promised.

The complaint seeks to cut off claims by what it calls 'purported junior lienholders,' including construction manager Strategic Group Builders, and 'purported contract purchasers' of condo units, arguing that the mortgage is superior to other interests arising from the borrower. Strategic Group filed its own suit on July 6, seeking $24.1 million it says it was not paid, plus interest, costs and fees, and seeking to foreclose a lien it recorded against the property. It named Via Mizner Owner III and four project lenders, which it says stopped making monthly payments to it without notice. The Coastal Star also reported that unit buyers have pursued their deposits in separate cases, and that at least nine have settled.

Separately, in a case concerning the adjoining Mandarin Oriental hotel, Penn-Florida affiliates Via Mizner Owner II and Via Mizner Pledgor II filed for Chapter 11 in December 2025 after failing to repay about $210 million to lenders at maturity, The Coastal Star reported. The auction of that property was moved to August 14, 2026, with a sale hearing set for August 21. The Coastal Star did not report a response from Penn-Florida to the foreclosure complaint.
