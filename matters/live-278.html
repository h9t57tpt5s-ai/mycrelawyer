Merchants Bank of Indiana v. GRV Jackson Park LLC: $6.4M Foreclosure on Three South Side Chicago Apartment Buildings

Merchants Bank of Indiana has filed a foreclosure complaint, reported on October 10, 2026 as filed earlier in October, in Cook County Circuit Court against borrower GRV Jackson Park LLC, seeking to foreclose on three apartment buildings in Chicago's South Shore and Jackson Park areas. The suit covers two loan facilities totaling $6.4 million made in June 2024, on which the borrower allegedly stopped making monthly payments in March 2025. The complaint also names the venture's two principals under their unconditional guaranties and seeks to hold them liable for any deficiency.

Why it matters

The case adds to a series of creditor actions against one South Side multifamily portfolio whose owners are already suing each other. It shows how a lender's collateral can deteriorate when an owner group breaks down: the complaint describes sewage backups, fire damage and a city-ordered evacuation, and the lender has been paying for boiler replacements itself. It also comes from a lender that has disclosed large multifamily charge-offs tied to suspected mortgage fraud, which makes the case a useful marker for distressed Chicago apartment debt.

Merchants Bank of Indiana has sued in the Circuit Court of Cook County to foreclose on three South Side Chicago apartment properties owned by GRV Jackson Park LLC. The properties are 6750-52 South Merrill Avenue (also known as 2116-20 East 68th Street), 6952-58 South Paxton Avenue and 6920-30 South Oglesby Avenue, in the South Shore and Jackson Park neighborhoods. The Real Deal reported the suit on October 10, 2026 and said it was filed earlier in October. The article does not give an exact filing date.
