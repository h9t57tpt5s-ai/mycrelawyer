C.D. Smith Construction, Inc. v. The Edison SPE LLC (Milwaukee Mass-Timber Tower Construction-Lien Foreclosure)

Lending & Foreclosure Ruling Issued
Date
June 29, 2026
Added to tracker
September 1, 2026
Court or forum
Milwaukee County Circuit Court
State
Wisconsin
Presiding judge
Glenn H. Yamahiro
Amount / scale
$11.3M default judgment on a $13.67M unpaid construction claim
Parties
Plaintiff: C.D. Smith Construction Inc.Defendant: The Edison SPEDefendant: The Edison Project LLC

Milwaukee County Circuit Court Judge Glenn H. Yamahiro entered a default judgment of more than $11.3 million on June 29, 2026 in favor of general contractor C.D. Smith Construction against The Edison SPE LLC, a Neutral affiliate, clearing the way to foreclose on a construction lien against the stalled 31-story Edison mass-timber tower site in downtown Milwaukee. The default arose after Neutral CEO Nate Helbach filed a pro se mediation/arbitration request the court struck because a corporation cannot represent itself in litigation.

Why it matters

The ruling gives C.D. Smith a direct path to force a sale of the Edison site via lien foreclosure rather than standing as an unsecured creditor, and is a reminder that developers must respond to litigation through licensed counsel or risk default judgment on the full amount claimed regardless of the underlying merits.

Original source ↗

The Edison, a planned 31-story, 357-unit mass-timber apartment tower in downtown Milwaukee once billed as poised to become the tallest building of its kind in the country, has become a cautionary tale in construction-lending and lien litigation. On June 29, 2026, Milwaukee County Circuit Court Judge Glenn H. Yamahiro entered a default judgment of more than $11.3 million against The Edison SPE LLC, an affiliate of Madison-based developer Neutral, clearing the way for general contractor C.D. Smith Construction to move toward foreclosing on the project's construction lien and forcing a sale of the site.
