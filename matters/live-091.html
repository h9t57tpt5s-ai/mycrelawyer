New York-based Mack Real Estate Group sued in Denver District Court on September 3, 2026 to have a receiver appointed for X Denver, a 12-story apartment building near Coors Field, alleging the $170 million loan on the property has been in default since it matured in January 2025, BusinessDen reported. Why it matters

The order hands day-to-day control of a five-year-old building to a receiver that may sell it with the lender's approval, more than 18 months after the loan matured. The same receiver also oversees Republic Plaza in Denver.

Full write-up

X Denver, a 12-story apartment complex that opened in 2021, is the subject of a receivership suit by its lender, BusinessDen reported. Its then-owner, Chicago-based The X Co., took out a $170 million loan on the property from New York's Mack Real Estate Group in January 2022. On Thursday, September 3, 2026, Mack sued in Denver District Court to have a receiver appointed, alleging the note has been in default since it matured in January 2025. BusinessDen said the current owner is unclear; Bisnow describes a 455-unit building at 3000 and 3100 Inca St., near Coors Field.

Mack asked the court to appoint Trigild IVL LLC, and Denver District Court Judge Heidi Kutcher did so on Friday, October 9, Bisnow reported, citing the Denver Business Journal. Representatives of X Denver did not appear in court and had not responded to the lender's complaint. Under the order, they had three business days to give Trigild access to the building and to documents including current leases. Trigild will manage the property and may sell it if Mack approves, according to the DBJ.

Trigild, which specializes in court-appointed receiverships, is also the receiver of Republic Plaza in Denver. Bisnow reported that The X Co. also defaulted on a loan tied to a Phoenix property, and that debt on the Phoenix and Denver properties was listed for sale in May 2025.
