GWIP Land Company LLC v. Board of County Commissioners of Weld County: Neighboring Industrial Park Owner Seeks Reversal of GlobalAI Data Center Approval

Broe Cos. affiliates that own the Great Western Industrial Park sued to reverse Weld County's approval of GlobalAI Cloud's data center on the 438-acre former Carestream Health property.

Affiliates of Denver-based Broe Cos. that own the roughly 3,000-acre Great Western Industrial Park filed a 24-page complaint in Weld District Court (Case No. 2026CV31187) on October 6, 2026. They ask the court to review and reverse the Weld County commissioners' September approval of a use-by-special-review permit for GlobalAI Cloud Inc.'s data center on the 438-acre former Carestream Health property. The suit alleges improper commissioner intervention, rule changes tailored to the project, open-meetings violations and unenforced stop-work orders.

Why it matters

Most data center land-use suits pit residents against a project or a developer against a moratorium; this one is a commercial neighbor, the owner of a major industrial park, using the land-use review process to challenge a competing user's approval. The complaint's focus on power and water capacity, a code amendment written in anticipation of the project and a negotiated non-enforcement of stop-work orders gives a roadmap for how approvals of power-hungry facilities can be attacked. Industrial landowners, data center sponsors and their lenders should expect approvals obtained on accelerated or customized tracks to draw this kind of challenge.
