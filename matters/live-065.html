In re Palmer Center Foreclosure — Wells Fargo Tower & FirstBank Building (Colorado Springs Public Trustee Foreclosure)

El Paso County Public Trustee's Office records show foreclosure notices filed against RPalmer LLC, Overland Palmer LLC, and Palmer Center Owner LLC — Delaware entities controlled by California-based real estate investment and management companies — over a loan with an unpaid principal balance of roughly $59 million against an original $74 million facility. The filings cover downtown Colorado Springs' two tallest buildings, the Wells Fargo Tower and FirstBank Building at the Palmer Center complex, plus several ancillary business-suite properties reachable through either tower.

Why it matters

A marquee, long-tenured downtown office complex defaulting on debt service — rather than a commodity suburban asset — signals that post-pandemic office distress has moved beyond the most obviously troubled properties. Owners, lenders, and asset managers with loans maturing in secondary downtown markets should treat trophy status and blue-chip anchor tenancies as no longer sufficient on their own to guarantee refinancing in the current rate environment.

Original source ↗

Downtown Colorado Springs' two tallest and most recognizable office buildings — the Wells Fargo Tower at 90 S. Cascade Avenue and the neighboring FirstBank Building, together forming the Palmer Center complex — are now the subject of foreclosure proceedings after their ownership group failed to keep current on a loan with an unpaid principal balance of roughly $59 million against an original $74 million facility. El Paso County Public Trustee's Office records show foreclosure notices filed against three affiliated Delaware limited liability companies, RPalmer LLC, Overland Palmer LLC, and Palmer Center Owner LLC, which are controlled by private real estate development, investment, and management companies headquartered in California. Several smaller business-suite properties reachable through either tower or the adjacent, separately owned Antlers hotel are also named in the filings.
