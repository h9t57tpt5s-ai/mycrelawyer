AZ 175th Street LLC, which owns a mixed-use building at 91-10/91-14 175th Street in Jamaica, Queens, filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition in the Eastern District of New York on October 10, 2026 (Case No. 26-44763). The petition reports about $14 million in assets and $12.6 million in liabilities. It is the owner's second Chapter 11 case: the first, filed in May 2025 as a single-asset real estate case, was dismissed in October 2025 on the secured creditor's motion, and the order barred the debtor from filing again for 180 days.

Why it matters

A repeat filing by a single-asset owner whose first case ended in a consensual dismissal with a refiling bar shows how borrowers use the automatic stay to interrupt a lender's enforcement again once the bar expires. Lenders holding loans on small New York mixed-use and multifamily assets should expect a renewed bad-faith dismissal or stay-relief motion. The first case's history should strengthen that motion, but a second round still means more delay and more legal costs.
