A Criterion Real Estate Capital affiliate filed a foreclosure notice for 450 Fifth St. NW, a D.C. office building slated for residential conversion, saying the owner owes $166.6 million.

An affiliate of noteholder Criterion Real Estate Capital filed a foreclosure notice on September 28, 2026 for 450 Fifth St. NW, a 539,478-square-foot Brutalist office building near Judiciary Square in Washington, D.C., the Washington Business Journal reported, according to Connect CRE. The notice says the owner, Judiciary Plaza LLC, owes $166.6 million on a $177.5 million note from 2007, and sets an October 28, 2026 foreclosure auction at the D.C. office of Alex Cooper Auctioneers. The building had been slated for conversion into 500 residential units.

Why it matters

The notice puts an office building slated for a 500-unit residential conversion on a path to a foreclosure auction. Nearly two decades after the 2007 note, the notice says $166.6 million of the original $177.5 million is still owed.
