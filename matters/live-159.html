Groton Housing Authority v. Cappelletti (Ponzi-Like Redevelopment Fraud & Asset-Seizure Motion)

REIT & Securities Pending
Date
March 13, 2025
Added to tracker
September 19, 2026
Court or forum
Connecticut Superior Court, Complex Litigation Docket, Hartford (transferred from New London Superior Court)
State
Connecticut
Presiding judge
Scott Chadwick
Amount / scale
GHA alleges at least $2M in misappropriated assets tied to Cappelletti's tenure; the collapsed underlying redevelopment plan involved a proposed $750M bond issuance for a ~$4B project
Parties
Plaintiff: Groton Housing AuthorityPlaintiff: Greater Groton Realty Corp.Defendant: All Points Employer SolutionsDefendant: Clermont & Associates

The Groton Housing Authority and its nonprofit development arm, Greater Groton Realty Corp., sued former executive director Robert Cappelletti and former property manager Jamie Lee in February 2025, alleging fraud, civil conspiracy, and breach of fiduciary duty tied to a scheme that allegedly used a look-alike shell LLC to divert assets from the agency's legitimate redevelopment vehicle. The case has since escalated: the Housing Authority has moved for a prejudgment remedy to attach Middlebury, Connecticut homes owned by Cappelletti and his brother David, with a hearing set for October 20, 2026.

Why it matters

Shows how a single controlling executive at a public housing authority can imperil a large bond-financed redevelopment plan through an alleged look-alike-entity diversion scheme, and illustrates the aggressive asset-preservation tools, including an early injunction and a prejudgment attachment of personal real estate, available to a defrauded public agency pursuing recovery.
