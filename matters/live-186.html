The $49.7 million CMBS loan on the 251-key Embassy Suites by Hilton Portland Airport moved to special servicing after it missed its August 2026 maturity date, Connect CRE reported. Why it matters

Full write-up

The $49.7 million commercial mortgage-backed securities loan on the Embassy Suites by Hilton Portland Airport, a 251-key hotel next to Portland International Airport, moved to special servicing after missing its August 2026 maturity date, Connect CRE reported in its September 24, 2026 lender roundup. The loan is held in two 2016 securitizations, GSMS 2016-GS3 and GSMS 2016-GS4.

According to Connect CRE, the loan remained current for its entire term despite years of debt service coverage below 1.00x. The Registry, in a September 22, 2026 report, said the sponsor had propped the hotel up for years and that the maturity default hands Rialto Capital Advisors a workout on a loan that barely clears breakeven. KBRA, in an October 9, 2026 surveillance release on GSMS 2016-GS4, said that deal's $23.8 million portion transferred to special servicing on September 11, 2026.

No foreclosure complaint or receivership request had been reported, and the sources reviewed did not name the borrower or sponsor.
