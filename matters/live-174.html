Full write-up

Cogent Bank has filed a foreclosure suit in Monroe County Supreme Court over the Wyndham Rochester Downtown hotel on State Street, 13WHAM News reported on September 1, 2026. The defendants include the hotel owner's companies, Horizon Edge Hospitality LLC and Horizon Edge Capital LP, and several other parties with financial interests in the property.

According to the complaint as described by WHAM, Cogent made the borrowers a $5 million loan backed by the U.S. Small Business Administration and an $850,000 conventional loan. The bank alleges the borrowers defaulted by failing to make required payments and to maintain the business. The parties signed a second forbearance agreement in May, and Cogent alleges the borrowers later defaulted on that agreement as well. The bank says it was owed $6,347,940.30 as of July 7, with interest and other charges continuing to accrue.

Cogent asks the court to let it foreclose on the mortgages and sell the property at 32-70 State St., and to appoint a receiver to take possession of and protect the property while the case is pending. The suit follows the City of Rochester's decision in June to shut the hotel after citing 13 open fire code violations and 10 open building code violations. The city rescinded the certificate of occupancy and said the hotel could not reopen until the violations were corrected and a new certificate was issued. WHAM did not report a response from the owners.
