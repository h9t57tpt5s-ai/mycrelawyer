Fairlane Town Center, the Dearborn, Michigan mall owned by New York-based Kohan Retail Investment Group, has been in receivership since January 2026, the Detroit Free Press reported. Why it matters

Full write-up

Fairlane Town Center, which developer A. Alfred Taubman opened in Dearborn on March 2, 1976, has been in receivership since January 2026, the Detroit Free Press reported on July 9, 2026. Taubman Centers sold the mall to Starwood Retail Partners in 2014, Starwood sold it to Dallas-based Centennial in 2022, and Centennial sold it in April 2023 to New York-based Kohan Retail Investment Group. In January 2025, Crain's Detroit Business reported that the mall had avoided receivership in a lender's suit for the time being.

Early in the week of July 9, one of Fairlane's lenders asked a Wayne County judge to declare the mall and its owner in default on a loan with an outstanding balance of more than $35 million, according to the Free Press. The paper reported that the owner has also owed millions in unpaid property taxes. The court-appointed receiver told the Free Press he is working on bidding and sale procedures for a future sale of the mall. A local attorney for Mike Kohan and the mall's main office did not respond to the paper's requests for comment.

The mall's anchors include Macy's, JC Penney, H&M and a Powerhouse gym, and many storefronts remain occupied with scattered vacancies, the Free Press reported.
