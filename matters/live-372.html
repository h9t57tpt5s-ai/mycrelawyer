JZ Investments sued Belpointe PREP, two related entities and a former partner, alleging fraud and a settlement cost it its stake in a planned 262-unit development near UConn; Belpointe disputes liability.

JZ Investments, Inc. sued Belpointe PREP, LLC, BPOZ Storrs Holding, LLC, CMC Storrs SPV, LLC and a former business partner in Connecticut Superior Court on April 24, 2026, according to Belpointe's quarterly SEC filings. JZ alleges the former partner acted fraudulently and Belpointe negligently in connection with a settlement agreement that it says cost it its preferred equity interest in the venture behind a planned 262-unit development at 497-501 Middle Turnpike in Mansfield, near UConn's main campus. Belpointe says it disputes any liability and has moved to dismiss.

Why it matters

The dispute shows how an investor in a development joint venture can lose its stake through a settlement it says it never authorized, and how the sponsor that ends up controlling the project can be drawn into the fight. Belpointe's pending motion to dismiss will determine whether the claims against it move forward.

Full write-up

The Hartford Business Journal reported on May 1, 2026 that JZ Investments, Inc., a Connecticut investment company, had sued a former business partner and several Greenwich real estate entities over the loss of its ownership stake in a proposed 262-unit housing development at 497-501 Middle Turnpike in Mansfield, near UConn's main campus. JZ alleges the loss was the result of a fraud scheme.

Belpointe PREP, LLC, a publicly reporting fund, describes the case in its quarterly SEC filings. According to its November 4, 2026 filing, JZ sued on April 24, 2026 in Connecticut Superior Court, individually and derivatively as an equity holder of CMC QOF I, LLC, naming Belpointe, BPOZ Storrs Holding, LLC, CMC Storrs SPV, LLC, which holds the Middle Turnpike property, and the former partner. JZ alleges the former partner acted fraudulently and Belpointe acted negligently, and it brings claims under the Connecticut Unfair Trade Practices Act and for breach of the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing, fraudulent transfer and unjust enrichment, all tied to a settlement agreement with JZ. It seeks restoration of its preferred equity interest, declaratory relief, damages and attorneys' fees.

Belpointe says it disputes any liability and intends to vigorously defend the case. It moved to dismiss on May 28, 2026 for lack of personal jurisdiction and insufficient service of process, and as of the November filing it was awaiting a hearing date. The filing also describes a separate suit by The Galinn Fund LLC, filed December 5, 2024, seeking to foreclose on a $3 million mortgage note that the former partner executed on CMC Storrs SPV's behalf in May 2024. Belpointe says the partner had left the venture and lacked authority to act for it, contends the loan resulted from his fraud, and says CMC Storrs SPV has filed counterclaims against him and Galinn.
