A Milwaukee County judge approved the sale of Southridge Mall from a Simon affiliate to a receiver-linked buyer assuming the $101.9 million loan and paying $7 million in cash, ending a CMBS foreclosure.

On October 6, 2026, a Milwaukee County Circuit Court judge entered an order approving the sale of Southridge Mall in Greendale, Wisconsin. The sale ends a CMBS foreclosure that began in December 2020. The 560,000-square-foot mall core is going from a Simon Property Group affiliate to Southridge Mall Member LLC, a newly formed buyer tied to the court-appointed receiver. The buyer will assume the mall's $101.9 million loan and pay $7 million in cash.

Why it matters

The case shows how long a contested regional-mall foreclosure can take: almost six years from complaint to an approved sale, even after the borrower agreed to a foreclosure judgment in 2021. The structure is also notable. A receiver's affiliate buys the asset by assuming the securitized debt instead of the lender taking title or holding an open auction, which gives CMBS holders and special servicers a model for exiting long-stalled retail loans.
