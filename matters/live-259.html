Shareholders filed a putative securities class action against Franklin BSP Realty Trust, Inc. (NYSE: FBRT) and certain officers, alleging the commercial mortgage REIT misrepresented dividend sustainability and overstated its business prospects ahead of disappointing fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 results announced February 11, 2026. The suit alleges violations of Sections 10(b) and 20(a) of the Securities Exchange Act; a related shareholder derivative suit alleging breach of fiduciary duty was filed in the same court in May 2026.

Why it matters

The case illustrates a recurring exposure pattern for commercial mortgage REITs: dividend-sustainability disclosures becoming the basis for securities-fraud claims once credit quality or interest-rate conditions force a dividend cut. It is a disclosure-controls warning for any CRE lender-REIT board, particularly one weighing an internal dividend reduction against public guidance already given to investors.

Shareholders of Franklin BSP Realty Trust, Inc., a publicly traded commercial mortgage REIT trading as NYSE: FBRT, filed a putative securities class action in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York on February 26, 2026. The complaint alleges that the company and certain of its officers misrepresented the sustainability of its dividend and overstated its business prospects in the period leading up to disappointing fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 results, which the company announced on February 11, 2026.
