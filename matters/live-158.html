Full write-up

Attorneys in a suit over the sale of Pavilion Park land in the western Des Moines suburbs had until Oct. 9, 2026 to file post-trial briefs after a bench trial in Polk County District Court concluded the previous week, Business Record reported on Sept. 16. A judge, not a jury, will decide the case; no timetable for a decision was available.

The plaintiffs in the equity petition, filed in March 2025, are Eagle Nest Investments LLC, Gratias Properties LLC and an individual co-owner, all minority owners of Pavilion Park. The defendants are William Spencer, Pavilion's manager and president, and TJK Investments LC, Wendover Reserve LLC and Landmark Brokerage Inc., entities he owns or manages. Spencer is also president and CEO of the development firm Landmark Cos. Inc. The defendants have denied any wrongdoing.

According to the six-count lawsuit, Spencer and TJK arranged to sell the land to Wendover, effectively buying out the minority owners over their objections. It alleges a 25.52-acre residential parcel sold to Wendover for $1.6 million, below its fair market value of $1.99 million, and that 126.52 acres intended for commercial development sold to Wendover for $11.4 million over the minority owners' objection, against a fair market value of at least $18.34 million. The suit also alleges Pavilion paid Landmark Brokerage an unauthorized, excessive broker commission. The property lies just south of Interstate 80 near the Grand Prairie Parkway interchange.
