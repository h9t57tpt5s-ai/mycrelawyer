An Arapahoe County judge on Nov. 16 appointed Trigild IVL receiver for Panorama Corporate Center, a six-building, 780,000-square-foot office complex in Centennial, at the request of Wells Fargo, trustee of a $133 million loan.

BusinessDen reported that Arapahoe County District Court Judge Joseph Whitfield on Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, appointed Trigild IVL to oversee Panorama Corporate Center, a six-building, 780,000-square-foot office complex in Centennial, Colorado. Wells Fargo, the trustee of a $133 million loan issued in January 2016 to Denver-based Sagard Real Estate, requested the appointment. BusinessDen described the complex as having defaulted on its loan.

Why it matters

The order puts a court-appointed receiver in charge of a six-building suburban office complex at the request of the loan's trustee. According to BusinessDen, Sagard Real Estate paid $190.6 million for the complex in January 2016, and Wells Fargo says the loan's principal was reduced to $74.5 million later that year.

Full write-up

A Colorado state court has placed Panorama Corporate Center in Centennial in receivership, BusinessDen reported on Nov. 18, 2026. According to the outlet, Arapahoe County District Court Judge Joseph Whitfield on Monday, Nov. 16, appointed Trigild IVL to oversee the six-building, 780,000-square-foot office complex, which BusinessDen described as having defaulted on its loan.

BusinessDen reported that the appointment was requested by Wells Fargo, the trustee of a $133 million loan issued in January 2016 to Denver-based Sagard Real Estate, which paid $190.6 million for the complex that month. The principal amount was reduced to $74.5 million later that year, according to Wells Fargo.
