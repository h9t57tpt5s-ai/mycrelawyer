Full write-up

Harbor Custom Development, founded in Gig Harbor in 2014 as Harbor Custom Homes, filed for Chapter 11 protection in December 2023 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Washington, listing $172.5 million in debt and $224 million in assets across four states, The News Tribune reported. In February 2024 the company said it had found no viable plan and would wind down by selling four apartment properties and assets in three other states.

One of those properties was Pacific Ridge Apartments, an 80-unit complex at 8445 Pacific Ave. S. in Tacoma's Fern Hill area, completed in 2022. The chief restructuring officer said in a June 13, 2024 declaration that the property was worth well below what was owed. Court filings put Sound Capital's construction loan balance at about $20.3 million as of June, and Pierce County assessed the property at more than $17.5 million.

Harbor Custom's second amended Chapter 11 plan, filed in late June 2024, named Sound Capital Construction Fund, LLC as the recipient of the property under a settlement agreement and provided that the related lien would be extinguished and the loan deemed satisfied. Sound Capital NW Holdings LLC, affiliated with Bellevue-based Sound Capital Loans, took possession through a $5 million credit bid on August 21, 2024. Sound Capital did not respond to the newspaper's request for comment.
