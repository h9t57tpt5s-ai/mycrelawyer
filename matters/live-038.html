UMB Bank, N.A. v. Public Finance Authority (Delray Beach Proton Therapy Center Leasehold Foreclosure)

Lending & Foreclosure Filed
Date
July 13, 2026
Added to tracker

Court or forum
Fifteenth Judicial Circuit Court, Palm Beach County, Florida
State
Florida
Amount / scale
$81.32M foreclosure suit (bond debt), per the South Florida Business Journal
Parties
Plaintiff (bond trustee): UMB Bank N.A.Defendant: Public Finance AuthorityDefendant (named as possible interest holder; not a guarantor): Tenet Healthcare Corp.Defendant (named as possible interest holder; not a guarantor): Proton International LLCDefendant (named as possible interest holder; not a guarantor): Varian Medical Systems

UMB Bank, as bond trustee, filed an $81.32 million leasehold foreclosure suit over the South Florida Proton Therapy Institute at Delray Medical Center, the South Florida Business Journal reported.

UMB Bank, as trustee for investors in two bond funds, filed a leasehold foreclosure lawsuit on July 13, 2026 against Public Finance Authority, the Wisconsin-based conduit issuer that issued bonds to finance the South Florida Proton Therapy Institute at Delray Medical Center, according to the South Florida Business Journal as summarized by Connect CRE. The $81.32 million suit seeks receivership and foreclosure of the property, The Bond Buyer reported. It also names Tenet Healthcare Corp., Proton International LLC and Varian Medical Systems as parties that may claim an interest in the center, while stating they are not guarantors.

Why it matters

Because the suit forecloses a leasehold interest, parties that may hold interests in the facility are brought into the case even though the complaint says none of them guarantees the debt. The receivership request would put the property under a court-appointed manager while the foreclosure proceeds.

Full write-up

UMB Bank N.A., as bond trustee, has sued the Public Finance Authority and managers of a bond-financed cancer treatment center in Delray Beach, Florida, seeking receivership and foreclosure of the property, The Bond Buyer reported on July 29, 2026.

According to the South Florida Business Journal, as summarized by Connect CRE, UMB, acting as trustee for investors in two bond funds, filed the leasehold foreclosure suit on July 13 against Public Finance Authority, a Wisconsin-based conduit issuer that issued bonds to finance the proton therapy center, which is mostly used for cancer treatment. The Business Journal reported that the South Florida Proton Therapy Institute and its equipment at Delray Medical Center could be seized in the $81.32 million suit. The complaint also names Tenet Healthcare Corp., Proton International LLC and Varian Medical Systems as parties that may claim an interest in the center but are not guarantors.
