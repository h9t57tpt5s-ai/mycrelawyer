The single-asset owner of The Travis, a 354-unit apartment community near Frisco, filed Chapter 11 listing more than $50 million in debt, with ACORE, which financed the 2022 purchase, as a creditor.

Travis Property Owner LLC, a single-asset real estate entity that owns The Travis apartment community at 900 Gordon Heights Lane in Lakewood Village, Texas, filed a Chapter 11 petition minutes after midnight on October 6, 2026. Law360 reported that the debtor listed more than $50 million in debt, and the petition puts both assets and liabilities in the $50 million to $100 million range. Acore Capital Mortgage, L.P. appeared as a creditor the same day (a 2022 trade report says ACORE Capital financed the property's purchase with a $73.4 million loan), and a cash collateral motion and request for an expedited hearing were docketed on October 8.

Why it matters

The Travis is a 2021-vintage Class A suburban Dallas-area property bought in mid-2022 with short-term, interest-only acquisition debt, a financing profile that leaves little room when the loan term runs out. The case will test how much room a single-asset debtor has against its secured lender: SARE debtors face a 90-day deadline to file a confirmable plan or start paying interest, and the petition itself forecasts nothing for unsecured creditors. Lenders and owners holding similar short-term debt from 2021-2022 should watch the cash collateral terms and any stay-relief motion.

Travis Property Owner LLC filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Texas at 12:10 a.m. on October 6, 2026 (Case No. 26-90220). Law360 described the debtor as a single-asset real estate holding company tied to an apartment complex near Dallas with more than $50 million in debt. Bondoro's filing alert identifies the property as The Travis, a multifamily complex in Lakewood Village, and reports that the petition estimates assets and liabilities of $50 million to $100 million each and states that, after administrative expenses, nothing is expected to be left for unsecured creditors. The debtor's listed address is 900 Gordon Heights Lane.
