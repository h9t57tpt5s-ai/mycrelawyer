Full write-up

Charlotte Hawkins LLC sued Bar One Lounge South End LLC and its two owners in Mecklenburg County Superior Court, alleging they defaulted on a 10-year lease for a nearly 2,400-square-foot space, the Charlotte Business Journal reported in April 2025. The Business Journal describes Charlotte Hawkins as tied to Atlanta-based Portman Holdings, which owns the 24-story Linea apartment tower at 2161 Hawkins St. in South End, a building with ground-floor space for shops and restaurants.

Bar One announced plans in January 2023 to invest $10 million in locations in South End and Mooresville, including a roughly 2,400-square-foot space at Linea. In March 2025, one of the owners said he was not moving forward with the Mooresville location, and the Business Journal reported that the future of the South End restaurant is undetermined. The report does not state the amount sought or include a response from the defendants.
