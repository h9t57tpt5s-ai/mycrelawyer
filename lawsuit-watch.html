A case is listed only when both sides are businesses or governments and a party's name indicates a commercial real estate owner, developer, hotel or property company, or it is a real property foreclosure or lease case between businesses. That is a reading of the caption, not of the complaint, so some are missed and a few may not belong. Individuals are never listed. CourtListener receives only some new federal cases, and state courts are not covered. Orders and closings shown are checked daily for each case's first 30 days, and for as long as anyone follows it.
