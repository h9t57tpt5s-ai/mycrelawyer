Distress Radar · updated daily

Every commercial real estate distress signal, on one map.

Foreclosure postings, CMBS loans in default or foreclosure, real estate Chapter 11 petitions, New York City foreclosure-sale deeds, lender lawsuits and federal CRE suits usually live in different places: county clerks, city registers, SEC trust filings, federal courts. CREdocket reads them every day and puts them in one feed you can filter by state, property type and record type. Click a state or a tile to narrow it; every record links to its own page and source.

      Sources. Foreclosure postings: county clerk trustee's-sale notices in Dallas, Harris, Bexar, Collin, Fort Bend and Williamson counties, kept only when the appraisal district classifies the property as commercial (Texas Foreclosure Sales). CMBS: monthly asset-level reports CMBS trusts file with the SEC (CMBS Distress Watch). Chapter 11 and federal lawsuits: federal court dockets via CourtListener, real estate businesses only (Chapter 11 Watch, Federal Lawsuit Watch). Lender suits: the tracker's lending and foreclosure write-ups. New York City: referee's deeds after court-ordered sales (usually foreclosures) on commercial lots, recorded with the City Register (ACRIS); the public copy runs about two weeks behind, and Staten Island is not covered. Map positions come from the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder; records without a street address count toward their state only. Loan figures are the amounts stated in each record (CMBS balance at the last report, original loan amount in a notice), never estimates. Individuals are never listed. A posted sale can be postponed or cancelled; a filing is an allegation, not a finding.
