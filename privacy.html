Privacy Policy

Last updated August 10, 2026.

Overview

CREdocket ("CREdocket," "we," "us") publishes litigation intelligence content for the commercial real estate industry at credocket.com. This policy explains what information we collect from visitors and contacts, how we use it, and the choices available to you. CREdocket is operated in connection with the legal practice of Jeff Novel.

Information We Collect

Contact form. If you use the form on our Contact page, the fields you fill in (name, email address, organization, role, and message) are sent directly to us via your own email client — this information is not transmitted to or stored on a CREdocket server or database.

Newsletter. If CREdocket offers an email newsletter and you subscribe, we (or our email delivery provider) will retain your email address in order to send it, until you unsubscribe.

Automatic data. CREdocket does not currently use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels. Standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be recorded by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes.

Third-Party Services

Typefaces on this site are loaded from Google Fonts; loading a page may send your browser's IP address and user-agent to Google's servers as part of that request. Photography is sourced from Pexels and hosted directly on our site — no request is made to Pexels when you view a page.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential legal matters you raise with us, and — if you've subscribed — deliver our newsletter. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Data Retention & Security

We retain contact and newsletter information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law or professional-responsibility record-keeping obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect information in our possession, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from any newsletter at any time using the link in each email. To request that we delete information you've previously sent us, email jnovel@krcl.com.

Not Legal Advice; No Attorney-Client Relationship

Nothing on this site, and no information you submit through it, creates an attorney-client relationship. See our Terms of Service for details.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jnovel@krcl.com.
