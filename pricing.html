Questions

What happens if I use all my monthly AI credits?

Your Free-tier browsing, tracker access, and watchlists are never affected — only new AI analyses are paused until your plan renews, unless you top up with the $49/10-credit pack, which applies immediately.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel from your account settings and you'll keep plan access through the end of your current billing period. Any already-purchased one-time credit packs never expire, subscription or not.

Do unused monthly credits roll over?

No — the monthly allotment resets each billing period rather than accumulating. If you want credits that never expire, the $49/10-credit pack is the option for that.
