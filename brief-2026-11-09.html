CREdocket Briefs · Issue 8 · Week of November 9, 2026

21 new commercial real estate matters, November 9 – November 15, 2026

Every lawsuit, ruling and settlement added to the CREdocket tracker that week, grouped by practice area, with a one-line brief for each. Open any matter for the full write-up and its sources.

Zoning & Land Use (7)

Bluetriton Brands, Inc. v. Washington Township Zoning Hearing Board and Weis Markets, Inc. (Pennsylvania Court Splits on Shopping Center Gas Station Fight)

Pennsylvania's Commonwealth Court reversed a trial court's site-plan ruling but remanded Bluetriton Brands' groundwater-contamination challenge to Weis Markets' proposed Bangor Plaza gas station, leaving the special exception unresolved.

On Appeal · Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania · Nov 10, 2026

Allison Lauderdale v. Hinds County Mississippi Board of Supervisors, Soul City Storage, LLC and Soul City Energy, LLC (Solar Farm and Battery Storage Permit Challenge)

Mississippi's Court of Appeals affirmed Hinds County's approval of conditional use permits for a 5,800-acre solar farm and battery storage facility, rejecting a neighbor's claim that the Board needed written findings.

On Appeal · Court of Appeals of Mississippi · Nov 10, 2026

GSL Holdings, LLC v. Charter Township of Lyon, Michigan (Sixth Circuit Rejects Developer's Civil-Rights Claims Over Permitting Delay)

The Sixth Circuit affirmed summary judgment against a Michigan developer that sued Lyon Township under Section 1983 after permitting delays over a septic-system requirement derailed its build-to-suit industrial lease with Moba Americas.

Ruling Issued · U.S. Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit · Nov 9, 2026

Rubicon Real Estate Holdings, LLC v. City of Pontiac, Michigan (Supreme Court Ends Developer's Permitting-Delay Suit Over Collapsed Cannabis Project)

The U.S. Supreme Court denied certiorari, leaving in place a Sixth Circuit ruling that Pontiac, Michigan's permitting delays on a 26.5-acre cannabis redevelopment, which cost the developer $45 million in financing, were not a constitutional violation.

Ruling Issued · Supreme Court of the United States · Nov 9, 2026

AB Realty, LLC v. Scott Lane (Eighth Circuit Affirms Dismissal of Apartment Owner's Claims Over Omaha Code-Enforcement Closure)

The Eighth Circuit affirmed dismissal of a multifamily owner's Fourth and Fifth Amendment claims after Omaha closed its 84-unit Yale Park Apartments for code violations, even though the inspector's warrant application contained false statements.

Ruling Issued · U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit · Nov 6, 2026

Bethlehem Landfill Co. v. Lower Saucon Twp. (Pennsylvania Court Quashes Landfill Operator's Appeal Over Rezoning Dispute)

Pennsylvania's Commonwealth Court quashed Bethlehem Landfill Company's appeal over Lower Saucon Township's handling of its landfill-expansion rezoning request, finding the trial court's order was not a final, appealable judgment.

Ruling Issued · Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania · Nov 6, 2026

McCormick County Residents Sue Over 678-Acre Data Center Rezoning

McCormick County, S.C. residents sued the county and the Pollard entities, saying the county's September vote to rezone 678 acres for a data center violated state zoning procedures and the county's own comprehensive plan.

Filed · McCormick County Court of Common Pleas, South Carolina · Nov 6, 2026

REIT & Securities (3)

New Securities Suit Against Arbor Realty Trust, Inc. (Problem-Loan Disclosure Claims, E.D.N.Y.)

A shareholder sued Arbor Realty Trust and two executives in federal court, alleging they misled investors about more than $1.25 billion in troubled multifamily bridge loans.

Filed · United States District Court, Eastern District of New York · Nov 9, 2026

Miller v. Sunrise Realty Trust, Inc. (Stockholder Suits Over Southern Realty Trust Merger Disclosures)

Two Sunrise Realty Trust stockholders sued in New York state court claiming the REIT's merger proxy for its all-stock combination with Southern Realty Trust omitted material information.

Pending · Supreme Court of the State of New York, New York County · Sep 23, 2026

Deutsche Finance America LLC v. Bayerische Versorgungskammer (Delaware Contract and Defamation Suit Over $2B US CRE Portfolio)

Deutsche Finance sued German pension fund BVK in Delaware, alleging BVK mismanaged a nearly $2B US property portfolio and blamed Deutsche Finance for losses of up to €853M. BVK calls the claims baseless.

Pending · U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware (removed from Delaware Superior Court) · Aug 31, 2026

Lending & Foreclosure (5)

The City of Colonial Heights, Virginia v. Alpene Land, LLC (Virginia Appeals Court Limits Retroactive Tax Penalties on Delinquent Commercial Land)

The Court of Appeals of Virginia held a city may collect default statutory interest and penalties on delinquent commercial real estate taxes even when its own ordinance is defective, but can't apply a newly passed penalty ordinance retroactively.

Ruling Issued · Court of Appeals of Virginia · Nov 10, 2026

First Citizens Community Bank v. SA Living Fund LLC (Pennsylvania Court Upholds $3.85 Million Confessed Judgment)

The Superior Court of Pennsylvania affirmed denial of a borrower's and guarantors' bid to strike a bank's confessed judgment on a $3.85 million commercial mortgage loan secured by a Philadelphia property on Spring Garden Street.

Ruling Issued · Superior Court of Pennsylvania · Nov 9, 2026

Goldman-Affiliated CMBS Trust Takes Pittsburgh's Chamber of Commerce Building at No-Bid Foreclosure Sale

A Goldman Sachs-affiliated CMBS trust took title to Pittsburgh's historic Chamber of Commerce Building after its $1.4 million credit bid drew no competing bidders at a November 2 sheriff's sale.

Ruling Issued · Allegheny County Court of Common Pleas, Pennsylvania · Nov 2, 2026

First Western Bank & Trust v. Rabadi (Grand Forks Commercial Loan Guaranty Suit)

First Western Bank & Trust sued a Grand Forks guarantor for $7.86 million over defaulted loans secured by clinic real estate; the case moved to bankruptcy court after the borrower entities filed Chapter 11.

Pending · Grand Forks County District Court, North Dakota (removed to U.S. Bankruptcy Court, District of North Dakota) · Nov 2, 2026

Kaanapali Land Management Corp. v. Newport Hospital Corporation (Maui Infrastructure Contribution Lien Foreclosure)

Kaanapali Land's KLMC subsidiary moved to foreclose a lien on Newport Hospital Corporation's Maui land after NHC refused to pay infrastructure costs owed under a 2014 agreement.

Pending · Circuit Court of the Second Circuit, State of Hawaii · Sep 29, 2026

Commercial Lease Disputes (5)

Audrey Lynn Arcelli and Ms. Audrey's Crafting Emporium, LLC v. Ghasem Properties, Inc. (Kentucky Appeals Court Upholds Retail Tenant's Default Liability, Vacates Interest and Future-Rent Award)

Kentucky's Court of Appeals affirmed landlord Ghasem Properties' $131,855.91 default judgment against a retail tenant and its guarantor, but vacated the 8% prejudgment interest and post-judgment future-rent components and remanded for recalculation.

Ruling Issued · Court of Appeals of Kentucky · Nov 13, 2026

First Care Therapy, LLC v. Congress Ave Boynton, LLC (Commercial Lease Default Damages Reversed)

A Florida appeals court affirmed a landlord's default judgment against a medical-office tenant and guarantor but reversed its $85,782.80 damages award for lack of a properly noticed trial.

Ruling Issued · District Court of Appeal of Florida, Fourth District · Nov 12, 2026

Atwood v. Lamar Obie Co., LLC (Portland Wall-Sign Lease, Good Faith and Fair Dealing)

Oregon's Court of Appeals affirmed a $1,237,191.92 verdict against billboard company Lamar Obie Co. for breaching the implied duty of good faith by keeping an ODOT advertising permit from the Portland building owner who leased it the wall.

Ruling Issued · Court of Appeals of the State of Oregon · Nov 12, 2026

Illinois Appellate Court Holds Commercial Landlords May Assign Claims for Tenants' Unpaid Rent

An Illinois appellate court held that a commercial landlord's claim for a tenant's already-accrued unpaid rent is a freely assignable asset, reviving a rent-collection suit against a Chicago tenant that had been dismissed for lack of standing.

Ruling Issued · Appellate Court of Illinois, First District · Nov 9, 2026

Devosky Hood v. Barbara L. Smith (Commercial Lease Eviction Judgment Voided for Lack of Jurisdiction)

An Alabama appeals court voided a circuit court judgment against a commercial tenant, ruling the case never should have left district court for a $53,000 unlawful-detainer dispute.

Ruling Issued · Court of Civil Appeals of Alabama · Nov 6, 2026

Premises Liability (1)

Lineage, Inc. v. Altus Power, Inc. (Cold-Storage REIT Sues Solar Companies Over Boyle Heights Warehouse Fire)

Lineage sued Altus Power and contractor Pearce Services, alleging they re-energized rooftop solar panels against its request before the June 2026 fire at its Boyle Heights warehouse. Both companies dispute the claims.

Filed · Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles · Sep 10, 2026
