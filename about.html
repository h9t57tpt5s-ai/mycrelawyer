About CREdocket

Legal risk, translated for the CRE industry.

CREdocket exists to give owners, asset managers, developers, and REIT teams a fast, structured read on the litigation and legal issues shaping their industry — without wading through court dockets.

01

Who it's for

Portfolio and asset managers, general counsel and in-house legal teams, developers navigating entitlements, and REIT investor-relations and compliance functions who need to spot legal trends before they become balance-sheet problems.

02

How matters are organized

Every matter is tagged by practice area — landlord-tenant, zoning, REIT & securities, construction defect, lending & foreclosure, environmental, eminent domain, and lease disputes — plus status, jurisdiction, and financial scale, so you can filter to what's relevant to your asset classes.

03

Why it's framed this way

Each entry includes a plain-English "why it matters" note aimed at operators and investors, not just lawyers — connecting a ruling or filing back to underwriting, leasing, or portfolio strategy implications.
