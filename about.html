About MyCRELawyer

Legal risk, translated for the CRE industry.

MyCRELawyer exists to give owners, asset managers, developers, and REIT teams a fast, structured read on the litigation and legal issues shaping their industry — without wading through court dockets.

01

Who it's for

Portfolio and asset managers, general counsel and in-house legal teams, developers navigating entitlements, and REIT investor-relations and compliance functions who need to spot legal trends before they become balance-sheet problems.

02

How matters are organized

Every matter is tagged by practice area — landlord-tenant, zoning, REIT & securities, construction defect, lending & foreclosure, environmental, eminent domain, and lease disputes — plus status, jurisdiction, and financial scale, so you can filter to what's relevant to your asset classes.

03

Why it's framed this way

Each entry includes a plain-English "why it matters" note aimed at operators and investors, not just lawyers — connecting a ruling or filing back to underwriting, leasing, or portfolio strategy implications.

Important

A note on the content in this build

This is a working interface

This site was built to demonstrate the MyCRELawyer product experience — the design, the filtering, the timeline, and the detail views — end to end.

Most matters are sample data

Most case, party name, court, and outcome entries in the tracker are fictional and illustrative and do not refer to real litigation, real companies, or real people. Entries marked Verified Update are the exception — real, sourced developments with a citation to the original reporting.

Fed by a live research task

The data layer (js/data.js) now also receives real entries from a twice-daily automated research task that searches for CRE litigation news and appends sourced matters (source: "live") alongside the sample content.
