Lead case · Lending & Foreclosure
101 Hudson Street, Jersey City: $250M CMBS Loan Sent to Special Servicing After Missed October 2026 Maturity
Jersey City, New Jersey (no court case reported; loan in special servicing) · Nov 19, 2026 · $250M loan

Commercial Observer reported on Nov. 19, 2026 that a $250 million commercial mortgage-backed securities loan on 101 Hudson Street, a 42-story, 1.4 million-square-foot office building in Jersey City, was sent to special servicing after missing its October 2026 maturity date. The report cites an alert from Morningstar Credit and does not give the date of the transfer.

Why it matters: According to Morningstar, as relayed by Commercial Observer, occupancy at the building has dropped to 64 percent in recent months. Commercial Observer reported that the largest tenant, Merrill Lynch, has nearly 30 percent of the office space under a lease that expires in five months.
