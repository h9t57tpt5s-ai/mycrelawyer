Lead case · Lending & Foreclosure
191 Peachtree Tower, Atlanta: $175.5M Loan Moves to Special Servicing After Borrower Says It Can't Pay at Maturity
CMBS special servicing (no court filing reported) · Nov 19, 2026 · $176M loan

Bisnow reported on Nov. 19, 2026 that the loan on 191 Peachtree Tower in Atlanta has entered special servicing after the borrower said it could not pay off the balance at maturity. According to Bisnow, citing Morningstar Analytics, Rialto Capital took over the $175.5M loan the month before, in October 2026, due to "imminent monetary default." Banyan, the tower's owner, did not respond to Bisnow's request for comment.

Why it matters: A $175.5M loan on a 50-story office tower in Downtown Atlanta has moved to a special servicer after the borrower said it could not pay off the balance at maturity.
