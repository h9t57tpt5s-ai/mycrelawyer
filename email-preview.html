Deutsche Finance America LLC v. Bayerische Versorgungskammer (Delaware Contract and Defamation Suit Over $2B US CRE Portfolio)
U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware (removed from Delaware Superior Court) · Aug 31, 2026

Deutsche Finance America LLC and its parent, DF Deutsche Finance Holding AG, sued Bayerische Versorgungskammer (BVK), Germany's largest pension fund, in Delaware Superior Court on August 31, 2026, alleging breach of contract, defamation, deceptive trade practices and other claims over a nearly $2 billion US commercial real estate portfolio, Bisnow reported. Deutsche Finance alleges BVK controlled leasing, financing and capital decisions, mismanaged the portfolio, and then blamed Deutsche Finance for losses BVK…

Why it matters: The case turns on which side actually controlled decisions on the jointly built portfolio. A separate criminal corruption inquiry in Germany covers the same investments, though prosecutors say breach-of-trust claims over the losses are not part of it.
