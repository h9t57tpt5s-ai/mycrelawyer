Lead case · Lending & Foreclosure
American Dream Mall Locks In $850M Tax Cut, Beating Back Bondholder Challenge
New Jersey Tax Court · Oct 30, 2026

The New Jersey Tax Court ruled that the state's Freeze Act bars the Borough of East Rutherford from raising the American Dream Mall's property assessment for tax years 2026 and 2027 above the $1,653,960,000 figure a prior judgment set after a July 2025 trial. The Borough had reassessed the mall to $1,899,992,100 for 2026, and the mall's own bondholder trustee, US Bank and Trust Company, joined the Borough in opposing the freeze as amicus curiae.

Why it matters: The ruling confirms that a PILOT (payment-in-lieu-of-taxes) property is not exempt from Freeze Act protection merely because it does not pay conventional property taxes, and that a municipality cannot defeat a freeze with unquantified evidence of improving market conditions or its own routine annual reassessment program.
