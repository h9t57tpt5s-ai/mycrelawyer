C&B Development, LLC v. Maxatawny Township Zoning Hearing Board: Pennsylvania Commonwealth Court Blocks 709,000-SF Warehouse Because Its Only Access Road Falls Inside a 500-Foot Residential Buffer

On October 14, 2026, the Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania affirmed a Berks County trial court ruling against C&B Development, LLC. On October 14, 2026, the Massachusetts Appeals Court reversed a Land Court judgment that had let CRG Acquisition, LLC build an approximately 800,000-square-foot warehouse and distribution facility in Holliston without a special permit. On October 7, 2026, Florida's Third District Court of Appeal affirmed a Miami-Dade circuit court ruling in a commercial landlord's suit against its tenant, Valdo Auto Sales Corp. The landlord, 865 East 49th Street Associates LLC, had won liability on all of its claims, including fraud and unjust enrichment. But the trial court awarded only $76,199.37 in past-due rent for breach of the commercial lease and nothing on the other claims. The appeals court held that the landlord never showed any damages on the fraud and unjust enrichment claims that were separate from the rent award, so a zero award on those claims stands.
