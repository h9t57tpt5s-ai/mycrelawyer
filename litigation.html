The New Jersey Appellate Division affirmed summary judgment against a Berlin Township tenant that claimed its lease's right of first refusal was triggered when Gould Holdings, LLC bought the whole property; tax arrears were remanded for findings.

On November 19, 2026, the New Jersey Appellate Division affirmed summary judgment dismissing tenant 73 Dun One, Inc.'s claims that its lease right of first refusal was breached when Gould Holdings, LLC purchased the commercial property containing its premises. The Tennessee Supreme Court upheld summary judgment for the seller of a Hilton-branded hotel near the Nashville airport, letting it keep $18,917,500 in earnest money as liquidated damages after the buyer failed to close.

On November 16, 2026, the Tennessee Supreme Court reversed the Court of Appeals and upheld summary judgment for FWREF Nashville Airport, LLC, the seller of a Hilton-branded hotel near the Nashville airport, in a dispute over $18,917,500 in earnest money.
