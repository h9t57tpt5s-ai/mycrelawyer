Commercial Properties, Inc. v. Montgomery Civic Ballet, Inc. (Commercial Lease Default, Indemnity and Untimely Opposition)

The Alabama Supreme Court on October 16, 2026, affirmed a trial court's order striking as untimely a commercial tenant's opposition to its landlord's motion for partial summary judgment, leaving intact a $65,827.50 unpaid-rent judgment and a ruling that the tenant, Montgomery Civic Ballet, Inc., owes its landlord, Commercial Properties, Inc., defense and indemnity on mold-related personal-injury claims arising from the same shopping-center lease. On the landlord's cross-appeal, the Court reversed in part and remanded, holding the trial court could not simultaneously certify the partial judgment as final under Rule 54(b) while also purporting to dismiss the entire case.

$65,827.50 unpaid-rent judgment affirmed; tenant also held to owe landlord defense and indemnity on separate personal-injury claims
Oct 16, 2026 Supreme Court of Alabama

Commercial Lease Disputes Verified Update Ruling Issued

Salha and Abu Ali Cafe v. 2020 Harwin 59 Shopping Centers (Restaurant Lease Repair and Sublease Dispute)

The Texas Court of Appeals for the First District affirmed summary judgment for a Harwin-area Houston shopping center ownership group against a restaurant tenant's claims for breach of lease, fraud, DTPA violations and quantum meruit over disputed repair costs and a refused sublease. The court held the tenant's own lease terms assigned electrical, plumbing and HVAC maintenance to the tenant, that the tenant never gave the written notice the lease required before claiming a roof-repair default, that an oral sublease-consent promise was unenforceable under the statute of frauds, and that the lease's $522,000 total consideration exceeded the threshold exempting the transaction from the DTPA. The trial court's $23,580 trial and $15,000 appellate attorney's fee awards to the landlord were also affirmed.

$23,580 trial attorney's fees plus $15,000 appellate fees awarded to landlord
Oct 16, 2026 Court of Appeals of Texas, First District (Houston)
