## 1. A $260M Chicago office tower now worth a sixth of its loan

A Cook County judge has cleared the way for special servicer LNR Partners to take title
to Illinois Center, the twin-tower office complex on Chicago's East Loop, after ruling
against the AmTrust RE affiliate that has owned it since 2015. The $260 million CMBS
loan was underwritten against a $390 million valuation back then; the towers are now
appraised at just $66 million, an 83% drop. AmTrust stopped contesting the foreclosure
over the summer once it became clear equity had been wiped out. A Cook County sheriff's
sale is set for November 12, 2026.

For CMBS special servicers handling deeply underwater legacy office loans, this is
another data point for taking title through a sheriff's sale rather than extending
workout talks with a borrower that has already walked away.
