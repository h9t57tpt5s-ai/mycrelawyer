CREdocket Briefs · Issue 9 · Week of November 16, 2026

54 new commercial real estate matters, November 16 – November 22, 2026

Every lawsuit, ruling and settlement added to the CREdocket tracker that week, grouped by practice area, with a one-line brief for each. Open any matter for the full write-up and its sources.

Zoning & Land Use (16)

Emerald Valley, LLC v. Benton County Planning Board: Arkansas Court of Appeals Affirms Dismissal of Neighbors' Challenge to 209-Acre Glamping Site Plan Near Beaver Lake

The Arkansas Court of Appeals affirmed dismissal of neighbors' appeal of a Benton County Planning Board site plan approval for Contentment on Beaver Lake, LLC's glamping site on 209 acres, because no administrative record was ever filed.

Ruling Issued · Arkansas Court of Appeals, Division IV · Nov 18, 2026

Louisiana Second Circuit Affirms Shreveport City Council's Special Use Permit for Data Center at Resilient Technology Park

Louisiana's Second Circuit affirmed the Shreveport City Council's special use permit for a data center of seven 400,000-square-foot buildings on about 254 acres at Resilient Technology Park, rejecting a challenge by three individual plaintiffs.

Ruling Issued · Louisiana Court of Appeal, Second Circuit · Nov 18, 2026

ADP Little Rock Data Hub LLC v. Pulaski County: Federal Judge Denies County Judge-Elect's Bid to Intervene in Data Center Ordinance Suit

A federal judge denied the Pulaski County judge-elect's bid to intervene in ADP Little Rock Data Hub LLC's suit over a county data center ordinance. The company is seeking to build a $6 billion data center near Wrightsville.

Pending · U.S. District Court, Eastern District of Arkansas · Nov 17, 2026

City of Jarrell, Texas v. BE Theon East Partnership No. 3, Ltd.: Austin Appeals Court Affirms Order That City Perform 2008 Development Agreement's Water and Wastewater Promise

The Austin court of appeals affirmed a judgment ordering the City of Jarrell to invite and award bids for water and wastewater utilities to a 24.4-acre tract it annexed under a 2008 development agreement with BE Theon East Partnership No. 3, Ltd.

Ruling Issued · Texas Court of Appeals, Third District, at Austin · Nov 17, 2026

Water's Edge at Wind Gap, LLC v. Zoning Hearing Board of Moore Township: Pennsylvania Commonwealth Court Affirms Denial of Woodland Variance for Two-Warehouse Plan

Pennsylvania's Commonwealth Court affirmed the Moore Township Zoning Hearing Board's denial of a variance that would have let a developer preserve 55% of a 51-acre golf course's woodlands, not the required 60%, for two warehouses.

Ruling Issued · Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania · Nov 16, 2026

Dickard v. Jackson & Jackson, L.L.C.: Ohio Appeals Court Revives Property Owner's Zoning Challenge and Section 1983 Takings Claim (2026-Ohio-4530)

An Ohio appeals court revived a commercial electrical company's as-applied zoning challenge and federal takings claim against Butler County over an order to stop business use of a nine-acre, residentially zoned site it bought for $270,000.

Ruling Issued · Ohio Court of Appeals, Twelfth Appellate District (Butler County) · Nov 16, 2026

BTE Properties, L.L.C. v. Township of Parkman: Divided Ohio Appeals Court Enforces 1843 Public-Square Deed Restriction on Parcel Within Commercial Lot (2026-Ohio-4538)

A divided Ohio appeals court held that an 1843 public-square restriction, repeated in a 2018 township deed, binds a parcel inside BTE Properties' 2.6298-acre commercial lot. ABC Development had agreed to buy land containing the parcel.

Ruling Issued · Ohio Court of Appeals, Eleventh Appellate District (Geauga County) · Nov 16, 2026

Lenox Township 1,402-Acre Light Industrial Rezoning: Restraining Order Halts Planning Commission Meeting in Suit by Shoreline Steel Supply and Landowners

A Lenox Township planning meeting set for Nov. 16 on rezoning roughly 1,402 acres to light industrial use was canceled after a Macomb County judge issued a restraining order, The Detroit News reported. A hearing is set for Nov. 24.

Pending · Macomb County Circuit Court, Michigan · Nov 16, 2026

Harpers Ferry Residents v. West Virginia Department of Commerce Officials (Supreme Court Upholds Tourism District Law Exempting $138M Hill Top House Hotel From Local Zoning)

West Virginia's high court rejected residents' constitutional challenge to the 2020 law exempting large tourism projects in small towns from municipal zoning and building codes. The Hill Top House Hotel redevelopment is the only approved district.

Ruling Issued · Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia · Nov 13, 2026

Exton Square Mall: Chester County Judge Rules for Abrams Realty & Development on Mixed-Use Redevelopment After Township Supervisors' Denial

A Chester County judge ruled for Abrams Realty & Development on its plan to replace the closed Exton Square Mall with townhouses, apartments, shops and restaurants. The township supervisors say they intend to appeal.

Ruling Issued · Chester County Court of Common Pleas, Pennsylvania · Nov 13, 2026

Stop PAX-1 Challenge to $15 Billion PAX-1 Data Center in Middlesex Township: Cumberland County Judge Hears Arguments, No Ruling Yet

A Cumberland County judge heard arguments Nov. 13 on resident group Stop PAX-1's challenge to the $15 billion, 693-acre PAX-1 data center in Middlesex Township. The developer moved to dismiss; no ruling was issued.

Pending · Cumberland County Court of Common Pleas · Nov 13, 2026

City of McKinney, Texas v. Cowtown Redi Mix, Inc.: Dallas Appeals Court Holds Chapter 245 Vested Rights Do Not Cover Ongoing Operation of a Concrete Batch Plant

The Texas Fifth District Court of Appeals reversed a ruling that Cowtown Redi Mix has a vested right under Chapter 245 to run its McKinney concrete batch plant indefinitely, and sent a dispute over rezoning notice back to the trial court.

Ruling Issued · Texas Court of Appeals, Fifth District (Dallas) · Nov 13, 2026

Prologis v. City of Trenton, Ohio: Developer Files Federal Suit Over Voter-Approved Data Center Ban

Prologis sued Trenton, Ohio, in federal court nine days after voters capped data centers at 25 megawatts, arguing the cap cannot reach its permitted, nearly 1 million square foot project.

Filed · U.S. District Court in Ohio (district not named in reporting) · Nov 12, 2026

National Hairston Clan v. Stokes County: Community Groups File Second Suit to Void 1,850-Acre Project Delta Data Center Rezoning

Four community groups and 16 residents sued Stokes County, N.C., to void its second rezoning of about 1,850 acres on the Dan River for the Project Delta data center, 11 days before a development agreement vote.

Filed · North Carolina Superior Court, Stokes County · Nov 12, 2026

Smyth County, Virginia Resident Sues Board of Supervisors to Void Data Center Zoning Ordinance

A Smyth County, Virginia resident's suit, reported Nov. 12 and filed in October, asks the circuit court to void the Board of Supervisors' September vote creating a special permit process for data centers.

Filed · Smyth County Circuit Court · Nov 12, 2026

Penn Hurst Holdings DE LLC v. East Vincent Township: Judge Lets Adjoining Farm Owners and Residents' Group Join Appeal Over Rejected Pennhurst Data Center

A Chester County judge allowed adjoining farm owners and a 950-member residents' group to help defend East Vincent Township's denial of a 1.9 million-square-foot data center on the 124.6-acre Pennhurst property.

Pending · Chester County Court of Common Pleas, Pennsylvania · Nov 10, 2026

REIT & Securities (2)

Holmwood Capital Advisors, LLC v. HC Government Realty Holdings, L.P.: Delaware Chancery Rules Government-Lease REIT Owes Ousted Manager a 3x Termination Fee

After a three-day trial, the Court of Chancery held a REIT that owns federally leased buildings underpaid its terminated external manager by using a 1.5x fee multiplier instead of 3x, but awarded the REIT $517,463.35 for an unconsented CMBS transfer.

Ruling Issued · Delaware Court of Chancery · Nov 13, 2026

Sachem Capital Corp. Sued in Los Angeles by Purported IRG Master Holdings Investors Seeking at Least $1.9 Billion and a Halt to Its Industrial Portfolio Transaction

Investors claiming interests in IRG Master Holdings sued Sachem Capital Corp. in Los Angeles on Nov. 3, seeking at least $1.9 billion and an injunction against its pending industrial-portfolio deal with IRG Global. Sachem says it has not been served.

Filed · Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles · Nov 3, 2026

Construction Defect (2)

Cadence McShane Construction Company LLC v. San Antonio Housing Facility Corporation: Texas Business Court Keeps Suit Over $45 Million Horizon Pointe Apartment Project (2026 Tex. Bus. 83)

The Texas Business Court kept a construction manager's suit over profits on the $45 million Horizon Pointe affordable-housing apartment project in San Antonio, rejecting the owner's argument that less than $5 million is in controversy.

Ruling Issued · Business Court of Texas, Fourth Division · Nov 17, 2026

Archangel Engineering & Construction Inc. v. Mellontikos LLC: Contractor Seeks Foreclosure of $603,654.68 Construction Lien on Olive Tree Greek Grill Property in Viera

Archangel Engineering & Construction Inc. seeks to foreclose a $603,654.68 construction lien on the Olive Tree Greek Grill property in Viera, Florida; owner Mellontikos LLC denies the amount is due and has counterclaimed.

Pending · Circuit Court for Brevard County, Florida · Jun 3, 2026

Lending & Foreclosure (16)

101 Hudson Street, Jersey City: $250M CMBS Loan Sent to Special Servicing After Missed October 2026 Maturity

A $250 million CMBS loan on 101 Hudson Street, a 42-story Jersey City office tower, went to special servicing after missing its October 2026 maturity, Commercial Observer reported, citing a Morningstar Credit alert.

Pending · Jersey City, New Jersey (no court case reported; loan in special servicing) · Nov 19, 2026

123 William Street: $140M CMBS Loan on American Strategic Investment Company's Financial District Office Building Enters Special Servicing

A $140 million CMBS loan on 123 William Street, a 27-story Financial District office building owned by American Strategic Investment Company, entered special servicing after an occupancy drop, Commercial Observer reported.

Pending · New York, New York (no court case reported; loan in special servicing) · Nov 19, 2026

In re New Grant Acquisitions, LLC: Chapter 11 Plan Confirmed in Atlanta Grant Building Case; Creditor UC Grant Building Holder, LLC Appeals and Seeks Emergency Stay

The bankruptcy court confirmed New Grant Acquisitions, LLC's Fifth Amended Plan on Nov. 19. Creditor UC Grant Building Holder, LLC appealed within the hour and moved for an emergency stay. Atlanta's Grant Building was marketed in the case.

Ruling Issued · U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Georgia · Nov 19, 2026

191 Peachtree Tower, Atlanta: $175.5M Loan Moves to Special Servicing After Borrower Says It Can't Pay at Maturity

Bisnow reports that Rialto Capital took over the $175.5M loan on Atlanta's 191 Peachtree Tower in October 2026 after the borrower said it could not pay off the balance at maturity.

Pending · CMBS special servicing (no court filing reported) · Nov 19, 2026

In re DK Arena, Inc: Amended Chapter 11 Plan Confirmed; K&K Developers Bid $56.05 Million at Auction for 53-Acre Former Palm Beach Jai-Alai Site in Mangonia Park

An order entered Nov. 18 confirmed DK Arena, Inc's amended Chapter 11 plan. The Miami Herald reported that K&K Developers bid $56.05 million at a bankruptcy auction for the 53-acre former Palm Beach Jai-Alai site in Mangonia Park.

Ruling Issued · U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Southern District of Florida · Nov 18, 2026

In re Kahn Property Owner, LLC (Oheka Castle): Chapter 11 Plan Confirmed After $72 Million Auction Bid by Gold Coast Property & Estate Management LLC

Minute entries in the Chapter 11 case of Oheka Castle's owner record a confirmed plan and more time for auction winner Gold Coast Property & Estate Management LLC to pay the buyer's premium on its $72 million bid.

Ruling Issued · U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Eastern District of New York · Nov 17, 2026

Panorama Corporate Center Receivership: Arapahoe County Judge Appoints Trigild IVL at Request of Wells Fargo, Trustee of a $133 Million Loan

An Arapahoe County judge on Nov. 16 appointed Trigild IVL receiver for Panorama Corporate Center, a six-building, 780,000-square-foot office complex in Centennial, at the request of Wells Fargo, trustee of a $133 million loan.

Ruling Issued · Colorado District Court, Arapahoe County · Nov 16, 2026

Nitya Capital, LLC v. FS CREIT Finance WF-2 LLC: Dallas Appeals Court Affirms Receiver Over Bella Vida and CDMX Apartment Complexes on Loans Exceeding $53 Million

The Dallas Court of Appeals affirmed a receiver over the Bella Vida and CDMX apartment complexes. Lenders with liens securing loans exceeding $53 million had intervened in a City of Dallas suit after a maturity default.

Ruling Issued · Texas Court of Appeals, Fifth District (Dallas) · Nov 16, 2026

StarTop Investments, LLC v. NICbyte LLC: Delaware Supreme Court Affirms Ruling That Lender Cannot Enforce Loans Against Bakersfield and Fresno Properties

Delaware's Supreme Court affirmed a Chancery ruling that StarTop Investments cannot enforce $20.4 million in loans against three Bakersfield properties and one in Fresno, after the trial court found the lender intentionally destroyed evidence.

Ruling Issued · Delaware Supreme Court · Nov 16, 2026

In re F-Star Socorro, L.P. (Five Star Development): Court Conditionally Approves Disclosure Statement, Sending Commercial Property Debtors' $180M Exit Plan to a Creditor Vote

A Texas bankruptcy judge let Five Star Development's Commercial Property Debtors seek votes on a plan that would roll $180 million of bankruptcy loans into exit loans and pay allowed unsecured claims in full.

In re Bloom Hotels 6060 LLC: Owner of Miami Beach's Sixty Sixty Resort Amends Liquidation Plan and Sale Motion; Judgment Holder De Paz Family Investment LLC Responds and Disclosure Statement Approval Is Reported

1440 Broadway Foreclosure: SL Green Special Servicer Sues Over $399M CMBS Loan on Amazon-Anchored Times Square Tower

An SL Green special servicing unit has sued to foreclose on 1440 Broadway, the 745,000-square-foot Times Square office building owned by StepStone and anchored by Amazon, over a $399 million CMBS loan, The Real Deal reported, citing Crain's.

Hotel Hayden Foreclosure: Judge Denies Summary Judgment Because $44M Note and Mortgage Were Not Properly Attached

First Tech Federal Credit Union Sets Foreclosure Auction of Westborough Shopping Center Over $32.4M Mortgage

A credit union lender has scheduled a Dec. 8 foreclosure auction of the 33.5-acre, Stop & Shop- and Staples-anchored Westborough Shopping Center on Route 9, citing a breach of a mortgage last amended to $32.375 million in July 2025.

Pending · Massachusetts mortgagee's foreclosure sale by public auction (Worcester County); no court case reported · Nov 11, 2026

American Strategic Investment Co. Foreclosure: New York Court Appoints Receiver Over 400 East 67th Street and 200 Riverside Blvd. Properties on $50.0 Million Loan

A New York court on Oct. 1, 2026 appointed a receiver over American Strategic Investment Co.'s 400 East 67th Street and 200 Riverside Blvd. properties in a foreclosure on a $50.0 million loan, over a condominium board's objection, the company's 10-Q says.
