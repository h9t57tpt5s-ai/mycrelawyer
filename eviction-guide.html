Why It Matters

Getting self-help or notice wrong is what turns a routine eviction into a landlord's own liability

Self-help can backfire badly

Change the locks in the wrong state, or without following the exact statutory steps, and a routine default can turn into treble damages (NY, NJ, MI), a per-day penalty (NH), or a total loss of your possession claim.

A defective notice restarts the clock

Notice periods, required content, and delivery methods are different in every state — and different again for nonpayment vs. holdover. Get it wrong and the whole filing can be dismissed, costing weeks.
